# 0007 — Three session types; competitor research; weekly stats loop

Status: Accepted (2026-10-10)

## Context

The creator works in bursts: shooting sessions of 5–10 reels, 3–5 times a month. Competitors lose less on
subtitles than on ideas, hooks and speed to trends. The developer wants the tool to watch competitors and
propose ideas and references to re-shoot for the creator's brands.

## Decision

Three session types instead of "edit this reel":

1. **Research & pre-production** (before a shoot): review competitors, bring 10–15 ideas with references
   ("what to re-shoot for this brand and how"), write hooks, scripts and shot lists.
   - Inputs: a starter competitor list per brand/niche from the creator (research expands it), the creator's
     «реф» saved collection, and a separate Instagram **research account** for browsing — never the
     creator's working account or a brand account, to avoid automation flags.
   - Analysis per competitor reel: transcript, hook in the first ~1.5 s, cut rate, subtitle style, length, CTA,
     visible engagement.
   - Adapt formats and mechanics; never copy a reel one-to-one.
2. **Batch edit**: the creator drops 5–10 sources in the evening; prep runs overnight; drafts and editable
   projects are ready in the morning.
3. **Results review** (weekly): the creator shares Instagram insights screenshots (views, completion,
   retention); findings go into the brand's "what worked" notes and steer the next research session.

## Consequences

- Research downloads public reels for internal analysis only; nothing is re-uploaded.
- The research account must be created by the developer or creator, not by the tool.
- Brand files grow a performance history, which becomes the main competitive advantage over time.
